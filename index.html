ATC Simulator

A browser-based air traffic control game. Vector arrivals onto the runway, sequence departures and keep everyone separated, or switch to the ground and taxi aircraft around a practice airport.

  • Approach radar: Mumbai (VABB) with crossing runways, or a single-runway practice field.
  • Ground: push back, taxi and hold aircraft at a practice airport.
  • 15 aircraft types, wake turbulence spacing, live wind, adjustable traffic.

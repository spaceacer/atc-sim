You are the ground controller: the tower lines up and launches departures waiting at the holding point of the runway in use, lands arrivals and turns them off the runway; you take them to their stand. Keep aircraft apart: closer than 40 m is a conflict (-20). Departure airborne +10, arrival on stand +10, go-around -5. Runway 27 departures hold at A5, 09 at A1; cargo stands are south of the runway, so cargo traffic has to cross it.

              How to play

              Approach radar

              Click an aircraft (blip, label or strip), then use the buttons over the scope or type. Chain clauses: IGO451 C PUNE C 11 T.

              C 240Turn to heading 240
              C 3Climb or descend to 3,000 ft
              C PUNEDirect to a fix
              S 210Speed 210 kt
              I 27 / L 27Intercept / cleared to land runway 27
              W / TLine up and wait / cleared for takeoff
              H / AHold / go around, or abort a takeoff

              Keep aircraft 3 nm or 1,000 ft apart. Orange: a conflict is predicted within a minute. Red: separation is lost.

              Ground

              Push departures back and taxi them to the runway; taxi arrivals to their stand. The tower and the air are automatic.

              PUSH EPush back, ending up facing east
              TAXI A5Taxi to holding point A5 (VIA A to choose the route)
              HOLD / CONTHold position / continue
              CROSSAsk the tower to cross the runway

              Scoring

              Landing, departure, arrival on stand+10
              Go-around-5
              Leaving the airspace wrongly-10
              Loss of separation, ground conflict-20
